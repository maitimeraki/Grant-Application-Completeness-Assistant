**Coordinator:** contact@aurorapure.example.org

---

## 1. Excellence of the innovation approach

AquaBright is an innovative water-treatment idea that uses advanced oxidation
to make industrial wastewater cleaner. The technology is novel and
world-first, building on our team's years of experience in chemistry. We
believe there is nothing comparable on the market.

Our solution will be greener than existing methods and we are confident it
will work well in industrial settings. We will test it with a local
municipality that has expressed informal interest.

Further details of the scientific approach, including the research
hypothesis and technical objectives, will be worked out during the first
months of the project.

## 2. Impact and measurement

The project will have a major positive environmental impact by significantly
reducing pollution in waterways. We expect substantial water savings and
energy savings, although exact figures are not yet known.

We have been told by several customers that they would be interested in the
technology once it is ready. We have a strong social-media following of
about 4,000 followers, which demonstrates our ability to reach adopters.

A monitoring plan is being considered and may be included in a later
submission.

## 3. Implementation and consortium capability

The project will be carried out by AuroraPure Labs with occasional help from
contractors as needed. Work will be organised into phases, and we will hire
additional researchers if the budget allows. Milestones will be defined once
the technical plan is finalised.

Risks are expected to be low because the underlying chemistry is well
understood. Contingency plans will be developed as the project progresses.
