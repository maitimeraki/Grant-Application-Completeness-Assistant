## 1. Excellence of the innovation approach

Textile dyeing is one of the most water-intensive stages of garment
production. Our project develops a closed-loop dyeing process that recovers
and reuses dye bath water, targeting a reduction in water use of up to 70%
per tonne of fabric processed compared with conventional batch dyeing.

The core innovation is a proprietary membrane-catalyst unit (MCU-3) that
removes residual reactive dyes from effluent so the water can be returned to
the dyeing line. Unlike existing filtration units, MCU-3 combines
electro-oxidation with a ceramic membrane in a single skid, which we believe
has not been commercialised before in the textile sector.

The research hypothesis is that membrane fouling can be kept below a
normalized transmembrane pressure of 0.8 bar over 500 operating hours using
our regenerated cleaning cycle. This has been shown in bench tests at our
laboratory in Turku at TRL 4. The project will scale the unit to TRL 6 in a
live production environment.

Three work packages are planned:

- **WP1 — Process validation (months 1–8).** Bench and pilot validation of
  the MCU-3 unit, led by VerdeTextile Solutions Oy.
- **WP2 — Pilot deployment (months 6–14).** Installation of two pilot lines
  at partner facilities, led by Baltic DyeWorks UAB.
- **WP3 — Dissemination and standardisation (months 12–18).** Publication
  of results and preparation of a standardisation input document, led by
  VerdeTextile Solutions Oy.

## 2. Impact and measurement

The project will cut water consumption by approximately 12,000 cubic metres
per year across the two pilot sites, corresponding to an estimated 40%
reduction in the water footprint of the participating production lines.
Energy consumption is expected to fall by 15% because warm water is reused
directly in the dyeing loop.

We have already served more than 10,000 small garment producers with
sustainable sourcing advice over the past year, demonstrating our reach and
ability to scale adoption of the technology.

Indicators and baseline:

| Indicator | Baseline (2025) | Target (2027) |
|-----------|-----------------|----------------|
| Water use per tonne of fabric | 90 m³/t | 54 m³/t |
| Effluent COD | 850 mg/L | 250 mg/L |
| Energy per dyeing cycle | 410 kWh | 349 kWh |

Monitoring will be performed using quarterly meter readings collected by
Baltic DyeWorks and reviewed by the coordinator. A monitoring plan is
described in section 2.3 of Part B (not yet drafted).

## 3. Implementation and consortium capability

### 3.1 Consortium

- **VerdeTextile Solutions Oy** (Finland) — coordinator, technology
  development, dissemination. Founded 2019. The company operates its own
  dyeing laboratory in Turku and has 12 employees.
- **Baltic DyeWorks UAB** (Lithuania) — pilot site host and process
  integration. The company runs a 40-tonne-per-day dyeing facility in
  Kaunas.

Both partners are established in the EEA. Verdetextile has two complete
audited financial statements (2024, 2025) and currently employs 12 FTE.

A draft consortium agreement has been prepared by our legal adviser and will
be signed by both partners after the evaluation results are communicated.
We expect no difficulty in signing before the grant agreement deadline.

supplier.
